Inside the app

Everything you spend, without the effort.

Keep scrolling. The phone follows you and shows the real screen for each part of the app.

Add in seconds

Type the amount. Tap a category. Done.

The add screen opens straight to a keypad, so logging a chai takes two taps. Pick when it happened, how you paid, and add a note if you like.

  • Big keypad, no forms to fill
  • Categories you can make your own
  • Works offline, syncs later
Scan to save

Snap the bill. Espere reads it.

Tap Scan and pick a bill or a UPI payment screenshot, or share one to Espere straight from your gallery or payment app. Espere reads the amount and where you paid, fills it in, and you just check it and save.

  • Scan a bill or a payment screenshot
  • Or share a screenshot to Espere from any app
  • Amount, payment app and shop filled in for you
  • You check it, pick a category and save
Home

Your month at a glance.

See what you spent this month, your daily average, what is left to spend and your recent entries, with one-tap repeats for the things you buy often.

  • Spent this month with a daily bar chart
  • Add again in one tap
  • Activity grouped by day, with search and filters
Budgets & reports

Know before you overspend.

Set a monthly budget per category and watch it fill up. Reports show income against spending and how your savings grow over the year.

  • Budget bars that warn you before the limit
  • Income vs expenses, month by month
  • Export your data any time
Split with friends

Trips, flats and dinners, settled.

Groups

One place for every trip, flat and dinner.

Each group shows what you get back or owe at the top, then every expense and payment, day by day. Add an expense or settle up straight from the card.

  • Overall balance for you, at a glance
  • Activity, Balances and Members tabs
  • Group settings for admins
Split a bill

Split it equally, by exact amounts or by %.

Pick who paid, then split between everyone or tap someone to leave them out. Shares update as you type and always add up to the bill.

  • Anyone in the group can be the payer
  • Equally, Exact or By %, with live shares
  • Every share adds up to the last paisa
Settle up

Fewer payments. Friendly reminders.

Espere simplifies who owes whom into the fewest transfers. Send a reminder with one tap, and mark a payment as received when it comes in.

  • Debts simplified automatically
  • One-tap reminders, sent as a notification
  • Record full or part payments
Invite friends

Share a link. Or find them by name.

Send the group link on WhatsApp, search friends by @username, or invite by email. You can see who has not joined yet, and resend or cancel.

  • Invite link that opens the app
  • Add friends you already have in one tap
  • Pending invites you can resend or cancel
