Group settlements

Intelligent Group Settlements

Split a bill any way you like, keep every share exact, and let the whole group settle up in the fewest possible payments.

1

Equal, Percentage & Exact Splits

Split any bill the way it was actually shared: evenly, by percentage, or by exact amounts for each person.

2

Automatic Rounding Correction

When a total doesn't divide evenly, the leftover is corrected for you so every share adds up to the penny.

  • ₹100.00 ÷ 3
  • 33.33 + 33.33 + 33.34
3

Greedy Algorithm Simplification

Debts are simplified into the fewest possible payments, so a group settles up in a handful of transfers.

  • 6 balances
  • 5 payments
