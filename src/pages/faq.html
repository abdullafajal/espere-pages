General

Espere is an all-in-one personal finance and expense tracking app. It allows you to track your daily spending, set up budgets, and seamlessly split shared expenses with friends or groups.

Yes! The core features of Espere—including personal expense tracking and group settlements—are completely free to use without any hidden fees.

Yes, Espere is proudly open source! Our code is publicly available on GitHub. We believe financial software should be transparent, community-driven, and free from hidden agendas.

Features & Usage

You can easily create a group (like 'Trip to Bali' or 'Apartment Utilities'), add your friends, and start adding expenses. Espere automatically calculates who owes whom and provides a simplified settlement plan to minimize the number of transactions needed.

Absolutely. Espere is built with an Offline-First architecture. You can log expenses and manage groups entirely offline. The moment you reconnect to the internet, the app automatically and seamlessly syncs your data to the cloud.

Yes, you have full control over your data. You can easily export your personal and group expense history and generate high-fidelity PDF reports for your records.

Security & Privacy

Total security is one of our core values. Your data is strictly encrypted both in transit and at rest. We utilize industry-standard security protocols to ensure your privacy is never compromised.

Never. As an open-source, user-first platform, we do not sell your personal or financial data to third-party advertisers or data brokers. Your money is your business.
